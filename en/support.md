# Support

## Contact
Email: [jjlab-support@googlegroups.com](mailto:jjlab-support@googlegroups.com)

When reporting a problem, please tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened, along with the app version, device model and iOS version. Send photos only when really needed.

## FAQ

### Are my photos or locations uploaded to a server?
No. Photos and trips stay on your device. Only representative coordinates are sent to Apple's location services to find place names. When you watch a slideshow on a Chromecast, photos go only to that TV on your Wi-Fi. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/en/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

### What happens to photos without a location?
In a trip, open **photos without location** to pick the place visited right before or after, **Choose from Places**, set a location on the map, or exclude them from the route. They stay in the album either way. In a trip made from a travel plan, photos taken during a visit TripRoute recorded join that visit even without a location.

### Places are split too finely or grouped too broadly.
Open **Grouping & Route** in a trip to choose how photos are grouped (radius or city/district, or the plan's visits for trips made from a travel plan) and, separately, how the map route is drawn. Renaming, merging and moving photos only change the photo places, not the route.

### Can I make a trip from a travel plan?
In the trip list, tap **+ › New Trip from Travel Plan** and pick a plan made in FlexTrip. TripPhoto makes an album of the photos taken during the plan and groups them by the plan's visits. When TripRoute recorded your arrivals and departures, photos without a location join those visits too. The visits are copied when the trip is made, so later changes to the plan leave the trip as it is. In **Grouping & Route** you can pick **Plan visits** or a radius or city grouping for the photos and for the route separately.

### How do I watch a slideshow?
Tap **▶ (Slideshow)** on a trip or on a place's photos, then **Play**. Photos play in the order they were taken, and whenever the place changes, the new place first appears on the map. Set one photo at a time or collages (automatic, 2, 3 or 4 photos), the transition, the time per photo and whether place and time show in **Settings › Slideshow**, and change them on the start screen. Tap the screen for close, previous, pause and next.

### Can I watch on my TV? (Chromecast)
If a Chromecast (or Google TV) is on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone, it appears under **Show On** on the slideshow's start screen. Pick it and play: the slideshow shows on the TV and your iPhone becomes the remote. Your iPhone sends the photos itself, so keep the app open while it plays. On a Chromecast, photos change without transitions.

### Why does the app ask for Local Network or Bluetooth?
The first time you open the slideshow's start screen, the app asks for **Local Network** access to find Chromecasts on your Wi-Fi. The slideshow on your device works even if you deny it, and you can change it later in iOS **Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network**. The Bluetooth purpose is stated because iOS requires it for the Google Cast SDK used for Chromecast.

### Can I go through my places one by one on the full-screen map?
Open a trip's map full screen with the button at its top right and tap **▶** at the bottom to go to the first place. **< >** go to the previous and next place and **|< >|** to the first and last; the map keeps the place in the middle.

### Are there ads or paid features?
No. Every feature is free and there are no ads. You can donate in **Settings › Support Development** if you like; donations unlock nothing.

### What about the ad-removal subscription I bought?
Every feature is free now, so you no longer need it. Cancel it in iOS **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions** (while you are subscribed, the app's **Settings** also shows a note and a Manage Subscription button). If you bought the lifetime ad removal, there is nothing to do.
