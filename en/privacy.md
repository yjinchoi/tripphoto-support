# Privacy Policy

<p class="meta">Effective: October 19, 2026</p>

TripPhoto (the "app") is an iOS app that builds trip albums and travel routes from the photos in your library. The developer (JJ Lab) has no sign-up and runs no server that receives or stores your photos or trips.

## 1. Photos and location
The app reads capture times and locations (EXIF) from the photos you allow it to access, to build trip albums and routes. Photos and results (trips, places and edits such as renames and merges) stay in the app's storage on your device and are never sent to the developer. Capture dates you change are also written to your photo library; iOS asks for your permission first.

## 2. Place names
To show place names, representative coordinates of places are sent to Apple's location services (reverse geocoding and map search), under Apple's [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 3. Sharing map files
When you choose to share, the app creates a KML, KMZ or GPX file on your device and hands it to the app or service you pick. KMZ files contain photo thumbnails. What happens after sharing follows the receiver's policies.

## 4. Ads
There are no ads for the first 3 months after installing. After that, unless you buy ad removal, a Google AdMob interstitial appears **only right before you share a map file**. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), device information, approximate location from the IP address, and ad impressions and clicks. Where consent is required (e.g. the EEA), Google's consent message asks first. You can change tracking at any time in iOS **Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking**. See Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Purchases and the shared free trial
Ad-removal purchases and subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store; the developer receives no payment details.
So that the free trial and ad removal also apply to the developer's other travel apps on the same device, the app stores the **trial start date** and the **ad-removal type and expiry** in a device keychain item that only the developer's apps can read. The item is not synced to iCloud, is never sent to the developer and contains nothing that identifies you. It may remain on the device after the app is deleted.

## 6. Retention and deletion
Deleting a trip or the app removes its data from the app's storage. Albums created in Photos are managed in the Photos app.

## 7. Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's information.

## 8. Changes
Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
